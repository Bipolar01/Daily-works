The Grid!

The Grid is a digital world inside a computer system. It’s like a virtual city made of light, data, and programs that act like people. Instead of humans, you have programs living there — they drive light cycles, fight in arenas, and follow commands from users (humans).

The Grid, a digital frontier. I tried to picture clusters of information as they moved through the computer... What did they look like? Ships, motorcycles? Were the circuits like freeways?I kept dreaming of a world I thought I’d never see. and then—one day… I got in.
